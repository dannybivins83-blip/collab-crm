Privacy in plain words.

MyRoofPortal is operated by Collaborative Concept LLC, Lantana, Florida. Last updated 19 November 2026.

We do not sell your details. Not to contractors, not to anyone.

We do not bid on your roof. We are not a roofing company and we take no commission from one.

Nobody calls you. No contractor is contacted and no lead is passed on.

Those three sentences appear on the comparison page, so the rest of this document explains exactly what happens to your information and where the promises are kept by the way the software is built rather than by policy alone.

What we collect

  • Your email address. It is how you get back into your workspace. It is required for nothing else.
  • Your name and property address, if you choose to give them. Both are optional and the comparison works without them.
  • The proposals you upload — the PDFs or photographs of the quotes your contractors gave you — along with whatever we read out of them and anything you correct or type in yourself.
  • Which roof system you tell us you have, because it decides which questions the sheet asks.

Who else sees it

Your proposal is read by an artificial-intelligence model, which means the document leaves our server. We think that is the single most important thing on this page, so it is the first thing in this section rather than the last.

  • Anthropic receives the file you upload so that its Claude model can read it and fill in the sheet. It is sent for that purpose and that purpose alone. Anthropic states that it does not train its models on business customers' API data.
  • Render hosts the site and stores the database and your uploaded files on servers in the United States.
  • Google Fonts. Our pages load typefaces from Google, so your browser contacts Google directly and Google can see your IP address and which page you were on. We get nothing back from that request.

That is the complete list. There is no advertising network, no analytics product, no data broker, and no contractor on it.

Who does not see it

Roofing contractors. The part of the system that holds your comparison is separate from everything else, and nothing copies a comparison into a sales, lead or contact list. It is not that we choose not to pass your details on — there is no route that would do it.

How we measure the site

We count page views using our own records, not a third-party tracker. Each entry holds the kind of page, the path, the page you arrived from, your browser's user-agent string and the site's own hostname. It does not hold your IP address or your email, and a visit to your private workspace is recorded against a masked path rather than the link that opens it.

We set no advertising or tracking cookies. The site uses a session cookie only if you sign in to a contractor account, which homeowners do not have.

How your workspace is protected

Your comparison lives at a long, unguessable web address. Anyone holding that link can open it, so treat it like a key: it is in the address bar and in any email you forwarded it to. There is no password, which is deliberate — it is how you get back in from your phone while a contractor is standing in your driveway.

How long we keep it

A comparison is useful while you are choosing and is dead weight afterwards. We keep a workspace for {{ retention_months }} months after you last open it, then delete it and the proposals in it. You can have it removed sooner by asking.

What you can do

  • Delete a quote yourself, at any time, from your workspace.
  • Have everything deleted — the workspace, the uploaded files and the record of them — by emailing {{ contact_email }} from the address you signed up with, or by writing to the address below.
  • Ask for a copy of what we hold about you, the same way.

We will act on a deletion request within 30 days and will confirm when it is done.

Children

The site is for homeowners arranging work on a property. It is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 13.

Changes

If what the software does changes, this page changes with it, and the date at the top moves. We will not quietly widen what we do with your information.
