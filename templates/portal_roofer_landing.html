Questions roofers ask

Does it say MyRoofPortal anywhere my customer can see?
No. The portal carries your logo, your colors and your company name. That’s the whole point of white-label.
Do I have to replace my CRM?
No. This is the customer-facing layer. Keep running your jobs wherever you run them — we import your list so homeowners get a portal either way.
How long does setup take?
Usually about a day once we have your logo and colors. Migration of your existing customers is included.
Does my customer need an app?
No. They open a link. It works in any phone browser.
What does “territory exclusive” actually mean?
While your Territory plan is active we will not license the portal to another roofing contractor in your city.
What if it isn’t for me?
14 days, money back, no argument.
