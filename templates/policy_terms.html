Terms of use kept short.

MyRoofPortal is operated by Collaborative Concept LLC, Lantana, Florida. Last updated 19 November 2026.

It is free for homeowners

Comparing your roofing quotes costs nothing. There is no trial, no card, and nothing to cancel. If that ever changes we will say so before you are asked for anything, and not after.

What this is

It is a reading aid. You give it the proposals you were handed, it puts them on one sheet, and it shows you which lines each one puts in writing and which it leaves open. The value is in the questions it hands back to you.

What this is not

We are not roofing contractors, engineers, lawyers, insurance adjusters or your agent. Nothing here is professional advice, and no line on the sheet is a finding about your roof, which we have never seen.

  • We do not rank contractors. A quote that says less is a quote with more open questions; that is a fact about the document, not a judgement about the company that wrote it.
  • We do not price work or tell you what a roof should cost.
  • We do not decide anything for you. Which quote to accept is yours to choose.

Check what the reader wrote down

Your proposals are read by software, and software misreads things. Everything it fills in is marked unchecked until you confirm it, and you can correct any line. Do not rely on a reading you have not checked against the paperwork in your hand. If a line matters to your decision, confirm it with the contractor in writing.

Use it for your own roof

Upload proposals that were given to you for work on a property you own or are responsible for. Do not upload anyone else's documents, and do not use the site to harvest competitors' pricing.

Your workspace link

Your comparison opens to anyone holding its web address, so share it carefully. We cannot tell who is using a link, and if you forward it, that is a decision you have made about your own information.

Availability and liability

The site is provided as it is. We do not promise it will be available without interruption or that a reading will be complete or accurate. To the extent the law allows, Collaborative Concept LLC is not liable for losses arising from a roofing decision you make, from a contractor's work, or from a reading you did not check. Nothing here limits any liability that cannot lawfully be limited.

Ending it

Stop using the site whenever you like, and ask us to delete your workspace — the privacy page says how. We may withdraw access to anyone using the site to misuse other people's documents.
